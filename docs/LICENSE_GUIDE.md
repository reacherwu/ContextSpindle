## 1. Executive Summary: The "Safety Green Line" vs. "Copyleft Red Line"

### 🟢 What is 100% Free & Permitted (Green Line):
1. **Personal & Solo Developer Use**: You can install and use Continuum forever without paying any fee.
2. **AI IDE Integration (Cursor, Claude Code, Windsurf)**: Using Continuum as an autonomous memory layer while writing your proprietary software **DOES NOT contaminate or infect your private codebase**. Continuum is a developer tool (analogous to Git, GCC, or Vim).
3. **Internal Corporate Deployment**: Your company can run Continuum internally to optimize CI/CD, store private architectural decisions, and accelerate engineering teams without open-sourcing internal code.
4. **Academic & Research Reproduction**: 100% free to benchmark, cite under CERN Zenodo DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22765180`, and study.

### 🔴 What Triggers AGPL-v3 Copyleft (Red Line):
1. **Network Hosted SaaS Service**: If you modify Continuum and expose it over a network as an online service/API to external third-party users, you must make your modified source code available under AGPL-v3.
2. **Cloud White-Labeling**: Big-tech cloud providers cannot package Continuum as a closed-source proprietary managed service without contributing code back.
3. **Closed-Source Redistribution**: You cannot bundle Continuum into a proprietary commercial binary distribution without an explicit commercial waiver.
