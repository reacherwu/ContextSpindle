# Decision rules

1. Evidence beats intuition.
2. Failed experiments are first-class results.
3. No cherry-picking or benchmark manipulation.
4. No baseline-free superiority claims.
5. No marketing claims in research documentation.
6. Research may invalidate novelty assumptions.
7. Adversarial work is rewarded for breaking the system.
8. Judge work remains independent.
9. Complexity requires measurable benefit.
